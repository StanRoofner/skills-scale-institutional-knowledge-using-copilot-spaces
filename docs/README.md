# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project management process with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, stakeholder alignment, incremental delivery, ongoing risk management, and continuous improvement. The lifecycle moves from initiation and planning through execution, release, and retrospective.

## Process Summary

- **Initiation:** Validate the problem and business need, identify stakeholders, define success metrics, establish an initial timeline, assess initial risks, and make a go/no-go decision for planning.
- **Planning:** Create and prioritize the backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, estimate work, identify dependencies and risks, and map milestones to a release plan.
- **Execution and tracking:** Manage work on the project board, use small reviewed pull requests, run automated and manual quality checks, monitor delivery metrics, and escalate blockers through the defined levels.
- **Risk management and communication:** Maintain the risk register, review risks and dependencies regularly, provide consistent stakeholder updates, and escalate issues to the appropriate leadership or security contacts.
- **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, rollback planning, and smoke tests before deploying; verify and communicate the release afterward.
- **Retrospective and continuous improvement:** Review what went well and what could improve after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents; track a small number of owned action items and measure their impact.

## Roles and Quality Practices

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, resources, communications, and documentation. Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the roadmap and backlog, and measure success. Developers build, test, review, and document solutions, while QA and testing roles validate quality and acceptance criteria. Stakeholders provide input, approvals, and alignment.

Quality is built into the workflow through unit, integration, and end-to-end smoke tests, CI linting and security scanning, manual QA when needed, pull-request review, and post-deployment verification. Teams use standups, weekly delivery or PM syncs, sprint or milestone demos, stakeholder updates, and defined escalation paths to maintain visibility and resolve blockers.
